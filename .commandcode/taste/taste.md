# User Taste

## Communication
- Writes in casual colloquial Indonesian ("gua", "dong") freely mixed with English tech terms (code-switching); responds well to Indonesian replies. Confidence: 0.85
- Gives very short, high-level directives (e.g. "improve UI UX", "retry", "gua mau relayout main menunya dong") and expects the agent to research, propose options, and drive execution. Confidence: 0.7

## Design & UI preferences
- Open to bold, opinionated visual overhauls — asked to redo "the whole theme" and accepted a distinctive characterful direction (serif display type, brass/parchment palette, expedition motifs) over a generic dark SaaS look. Confidence: 0.6
- Likes menu/home screens that feel like a themed experience (hero-driven "stage" layout with a strong call-to-action) rather than a plain form. Confidence: 0.55
- Reports specific visual defects in the rendered UI (dropdown overlap "tumpang tindih", clipped elements "ke potong") and gives blunt quality verdicts ("themenya kureng") — expects polished, defect-free UI, not merely functional. Confidence: 0.65
- Prefers spacious full-width layouts over cramped stacked/narrow columns for complex config screens (suggested full-width after seeing overlapping controls). Confidence: 0.6
- Likes rich, semantically-coded map/data visuals (asked for world-map country polygons tinted by each nation's flag colors). Confidence: 0.5
